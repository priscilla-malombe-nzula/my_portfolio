About Me

I am a Credit Scoring Analyst based in Nairobi, Kenya, with over 5 years of professional experience. In my current role, I design and maintain application and behavioural credit scorecards. My work spans the full model lifecycle, from data extraction, cleaning, and transformation, through model validation and back-testing, to implementation within decisioning systems and lending workflows.

I monitor model performance using industry-standard metrics (GINI, KS, PSI) and recommend recalibrations to keep scorecards accurate over time. I work closely with IT, data, and business teams to deploy scoring models and support risk-based pricing and segmentation strategies. I also build dashboards and reports that track score performance and portfolio risk, while ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements (CBK, IFRS9, Basel) and supporting the automation of credit decisioning processes.

Alongside this, I specialize in transforming complex datasets into actionable business insights across three additional industry areas: HR Analytics, Sales Analytics and Manufacturing Analytics. My work covers predictive modeling, exploratory analysis, and interactive dashboards, always tied to a real business problem.

I hold a BSc in Mathematics and Computer Science from Multimedia University of Kenya, a Data Science certification from Moringa School and a Data Protection certification from Strathmore University. I am open to freelance and full-time opportunities both locally and internationally.
