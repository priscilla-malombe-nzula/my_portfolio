About Me

I am a Data Scientist and Analyst based in Nairobi, Kenya, with over 5 years of professional experience. Before transitioning into data science, I spent three years at Equity Bank managing credit analytics for a KES 450 billion loan portfolio across 30,000 clients. That experience gave me a deep appreciation for what data-driven decisions cost when they go wrong and what they are worth when they go right.

I specialize in transforming complex datasets into actionable business insights, with a focus on four industry areas: HR Analytics, Sales Analytics and Manufacturing Analytics. My work covers predictive modeling, exploratory analysis, and interactive dashboards, always tied to a real business problem.

I hold a BSc in Mathematics and Computer Science from Multimedia University of Kenya, a Data Science certification from Moringa School and a Data Protection certification from Strathmore University. I am open to freelance and full-time opportunities both locally and internationally.
